Much of the work was SharePoint-based: intranets, views and portals built for
clients. Two projects stand out from that. For Philips I hand-built a YouTube
client for an early Smart TV, calling the YouTube API for data on a device far
more constrained than the TV platforms I would work with a decade later. For
Nordea and Varma I built interactive calculators for their websites — the Varma
one a pension calculator where users entered their details into a form and a
graph projected the expected growth of their pension.
