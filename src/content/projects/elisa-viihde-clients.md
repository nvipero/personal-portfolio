I joined the Smart TV team and learned the BBC TAL framework — native JavaScript
with its own conventions for TV apps — to deliver applications for Samsung Tizen
and LG webOS. As the teams were reorganised over the years, the role grew into a
broad client-side one covering Smart TV apps, set-top box configuration, widgets
and apps for several Netgem device families, and web development for
elisaviihde.fi.

Alongside frontend code I owned a lot of the surrounding plumbing: set-top box
configuration XML, GitHub Actions and Workflow CI pipelines, deploy scripts and
release work. One of the final major projects was leading the migration to
Elisa's single sign-on login — delivered into production and stable since,
despite SSO and authentication not being the team's home territory.

A large part of the engagement was maintenance ownership of older systems still
serving a live customer base. That included modernising a long-neglected
project's dependencies with the help of GitHub Copilot, cutting
Dependabot-reported vulnerabilities from roughly 120 to about 20 in a short
time — a backlog several developers before me had been unable to move
meaningfully. From January 2024 I also acted as Scrum Master for the team,
stepping up in a junior-heavy group when the previous SM needed to reduce their
load: facilitating ceremonies, mentoring junior developers and serving as the
team's central point of technical contact.
