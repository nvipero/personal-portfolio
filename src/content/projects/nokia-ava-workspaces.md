AVA Workspaces let Nokia and its customers provision and manage workspaces,
services and analytics workloads — including an Apache Spark–powered analytics
engine — behind one unified UI and API. I worked both sides of the stack: React
micro-frontends on the client, and the Node.js and Express microservices behind
the API gateway, including reading from and pushing into the platform's data
pipeline.

I contributed to the micro-frontend architecture as it moved from single-spa to
an iframe-based approach. The problem space was ambiguous and loosely specified
when we started, and turning it into working, delivered functionality was
genuinely the engagement's biggest win. The team was consultant-heavy and led by
Nokia management, working in Scrum inside a larger scaled delivery model.
