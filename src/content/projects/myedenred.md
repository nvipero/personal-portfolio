MyEdenred served several distinct audiences from one codebase: end users and
employees, the restaurants accepting the benefits, and the companies providing
them to their staff. The mobile app was built with Apache Cordova on top of the
same code. I built features across the end-user and restaurant layers,
including location-based restaurant discovery that used the device's location to
show the nearest participating restaurants.

This was the assignment where I first worked with React in production — picked
up on the job after a demanding hiring process, and ran with it. I also acted as
senior developer and mentor to the team's junior developers, in a small
international team working in English with Scrum and Jira.
