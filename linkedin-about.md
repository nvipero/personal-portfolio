I love the Internet, the good and the bad of it. I see the web as a critical part
of our society, our way of working and our way of thinking. It enables us to
enjoy life more. In my mind there is still no better way to influence the world
than to work towards a better Internet — twenty years in, that hasn't worn off.

My passion is building services that have real meaning to real users. JavaScript
and TypeScript, React, Node.js, HTML, CSS and pretty much anything around them:
I know a lot and I learn fast. I appreciate clean code and an emphasis on
quality, and I design with the end user in mind — including the users on a
keyboard, on a screen reader, or on a device that nobody is going to replace this
decade. I'm not afraid to fail, but I learn from my mistakes, and I take strong
responsibility for my own work.

Most of my career has gone into platforms other people find awkward. Smart TVs
and set-top boxes in your living room, hybrid mobile apps in your pocket, web
apps on your work laptop — usually long-lived production systems with hundreds of
thousands of customers, where shipping new features and keeping the older parts
healthy are the same job rather than competing ones. Somewhere along the way my
goal stopped being "become a UI wizard" and turned into something more useful: be
the person the team can ask, and leave the codebase in a state the next developer
can actually work with.

These days that also means mentoring junior developers, acting as Scrum Master
when a team needs one, and working out where AI-assisted development genuinely
helps — I use GitHub Copilot and Claude Code daily, with a fairly clear sense of
what they are and aren't good at.

My background is a mix of business and IT education, flavoured heavily with
self-taught skills from the web developer field of work. I still want to learn
new things and surround myself with smart people who can teach me and give me
fresh ideas.

And I'm still a total video game geek — the console generations keep changing,
the habit doesn't. I also like to talk about sports, and I've recently taken up
running, constantly pushing myself to improve. I've set myself a target for
spring 2027: a half marathon.
